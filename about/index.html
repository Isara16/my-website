Hello there, I’m Anahé Marchand, a graphic designer and project manager from Switzerland! I’m a curious person who loves music, craft beer and exploring the world. Right now, I’m looking for a new adventure where I can join a dynamic team, keep growing as a designer and contribute to meaningful projects.

I consider myself like a curious and easy-going person driven by a genuine passion for what I do. I’m adaptable and sensitive to the little things that make life interesting. I believe in staying authentic and positive while communicating efficiently. My inspiration comes from the world around me—whether that’s through exploring new places, grooving to a great tune or simply daydreaming. My work is all about blending these passions into something unique and beautiful.

My journey started at the graphic design school « Eracom » (Lausanne), which led me to an internship abroad with « The Gloss Magazine » (Dublin). After that, I spent five years at Dr. Gab’s Brewery (Puidoux), where I grew from an intern to both a designer and project manager. I’ve also been freelancing on the side, diving into projects that connect my love for craft beer, music, and culture. In the quest of meaning I gave my time as a volunteer firefighter in my local fire brigade (Vevey).

Feeling curious about me or my work? I’d love to hear from you! Whether you have a project in mind or just want to chat over a hot chocolate, feel free to reach out.
